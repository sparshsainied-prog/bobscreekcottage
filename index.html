Between Orange and Molong, Central West NSW

Bob's Creek Cottage

Green hills, peace and quiet, and the wood fire already burning when you arrive.

Then the sun goes down over Guanna Hill.

Light the outdoor fire, run the bath, and look up at the stars.

Inside

A tin cottage with a freshly renovated, character-filled inside. Two queen bedrooms, a lounge round the fire, and a kitchen with everything you need to cook.

Sleeps 5
Two queen bedrooms, plus a sofa bed in the lounge for an adult or a child.
Beds
Pure French flax linen, Australian wool blankets and electric blankets.
Warm
The wood fire, a split system in the kitchen, and heaters in the bedrooms. Orange gets properly cold in winter, and sometimes snows.
Kitchen
Small oven and air fryer, hot plate, microwave, full fridge, a pod coffee machine with pods, and a dining table. A Weber on the verandah.
Also
One bathroom with a shower. Washing machine. A cot if you ask. Wifi and a flatscreen TV.
Private
Its own fenced yard and garden, and its own driveway. Liana lives in the main homestead, not far away.

The outdoor bath

A clawfoot tub on a timber deck against the tin wall, looking out over the paddocks. The bubble bath and salts are there for you. At night the festoon lights come on.

"Highlights include the outdoor fireplace and the beautiful outdoor bath tub."

Follow the map on the fridge down the private laneway to Molong Creek.

The farm round you

Cattle graze just below the cottage, and sheep along the road in. Kangaroos and king parrots turn up often. From the yard you can see back towards Mount Canobolas and Orange.

The outdoor fire

Sit round it with a local wine, toast marshmallows, tell a few stories, and watch the stars come out.

Outdoor fires are for the cooler months. Fire bans are followed in the hot months.
